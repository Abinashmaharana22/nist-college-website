About the Institute

Learning that connects knowledge with innovation

NIST is an autonomous educational and research institution located at the foothills of Pallur Hills in Berhampur, Odisha.

NIST Institute of Science and Technology was established in 1996 and has developed into a multidisciplinary institution offering programmes across engineering, science, management, health sciences and research.

The campus combines academic spaces, laboratories, library facilities, classrooms, student amenities and research infrastructure to support academic and social life.

Mission

To build a research-oriented academic environment committed to academic excellence, fundamental research and innovation, while nurturing global citizens and collaborative engagement.

Vision

“High Quality Learning, Creative Innovation, Entrepreneurship, and Universal Partnership.”

A learning ecosystem where students can question, create, collaborate and turn ideas into meaningful outcomes.
